Perfect — here’s everything fully written out, step-by-step, to build your LIV8 R&D Credits landing page in Replit under your existing website liv8.co.

⸻

✅ GOAL

You’re adding a “Funding & Credits” page to your LIV8 website, and under that, a section for R&D Tax Credits that links to a dedicated landing page. This page will explain the refund opportunity and collect leads through a form.

⸻

📍FINAL URL STRUCTURE

https://liv8.co/funding-credits/rnd/index.html


⸻

🛠️ HOW TO BUILD THIS IN REPLIT

STEP 1: Create the Directory Structure

In your Replit project:
	1.	Inside your public folder (or wherever you serve frontend files), create a new folder:

/funding-credits/


	2.	Inside funding-credits/, create another folder:

/rnd/


	3.	You will place 3 files inside the /rnd/ folder:

index.html
style.css
script.js



⸻

🧾 STEP 2: Add the index.html Page

Paste this entire code into a new file called index.html inside the /funding-credits/rnd/ folder:
